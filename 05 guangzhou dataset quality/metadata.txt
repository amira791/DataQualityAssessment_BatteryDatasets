* time                -- timestamp of each sample
* vhc_speed           -- vehicle speed
* charging_signal     -- mode indicator (1 = charging, 3 = driving)
* vhc_totalMile       -- cumulative mileage
* hv_voltage          -- pack voltage
* hv_current          -- pack current
* bcell_soc           -- state of charge
* bcell_maxVoltage    -- maximum cell voltage
* bcell_minVoltage    -- minimum cell voltage
* bcell_maxTemp       -- maximum cell temperature
* bcell_minTemp       -- minimum cell temperature

opposite; the GitHub README and the reference paper are treated as
authoritative.)

============================================================
7. PHYSICAL BOUNDS (paper-derived)
============================================================

The following bounds are derived from the reference paper and the
vehicle pack structure documented in the GitHub repository. They are
used by the physical-plausibility check in the quality-assessment
code.

Pack-level:

* Pack voltage:     250 -- 450 V
    Implied from 91 NCM cells in series; typical NCM cell voltage
    3.0 -- 4.2 V gives ~273 -- 382 V, with tolerance for
    transient swings and pack ageing. Paper Fig. 2(d) shows the
    observed pack-voltage range.

* Pack current:     -200 -- +200 A
    Driving / charging operational range. Paper Fig. 2(e) shows
    the dynamic current profile. Positive = charging,
    negative = discharging.

* Battery temperature (max cell temperature):
                    10 -- 40 C
    Paper Fig. 2(h,i): "cell temperatures fluctuate between 10
    and 40 C".

* Ambient temperature:
                    0 -- 30 C
    Paper Fig. 2(c): "ambient operating temperatures range from
    approximately 0 to 30 C".

Cell-level:

* Cell voltage:     2.5 -- 4.2 V (NCM) / 2.0 -- 3.65 V (LFP)
    Paper Fig. 2(f,g) shows cell voltages. Some readings >6 V
    were flagged as errors in the paper.

* Cell temperature: 10 -- 40 C
    Paper Fig. 2(h,i).
