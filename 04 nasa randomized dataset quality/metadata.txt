============================================================
1. DATASET IDENTITY
============================================================

Dataset name:
Randomized and Recommissioned Battery Dataset

Provider:
NASA

Dataset purpose:
Accelerated life testing and degradation analysis of lithium-ion
battery packs under controlled and varied load conditions.

Dataset organization:
Each battery pack is provided as a dedicated CSV file containing
the continuous cycling history of the pack.

Main battery categories:
- Regular alternating batteries
- Recommissioned batteries
- Second-life batteries

The dataset includes both conventional constant-current profiles
and variable-current profiles, as well as batteries whose operating
conditions are modified during their lifetime.


============================================================
2. BATTERY CHARACTERISTICS
============================================================

Battery type:
Lithium-ion battery packs

Battery unit:
Battery pack

Pack configuration:
2S (two cells in series)

Nominal cell capacity:
Approximately 1.1 Ah

Nominal cell voltage:
Approximately 3.3 V (LFP family)

Pack voltage window:
Approximately 4.0 - 7.30 V (2 x cell 2.0-3.65 V)

Number of identified battery packs:
Multiple packs across regular, recommissioned, and second-life
testing categories. The regular folder (assessed here) contains
15 packs.

Second-life batteries:
Some packs are reconstructed from cells that survived previous
tests and are subsequently subjected to lower-stress operating
profiles.

Initial battery configuration:
Varies according to the experimental group and battery pack.

Battery chemistry:
Lithium-ion, LFP family. Detailed cell model number is not
provided in the dataset metadata; pack-level configuration and
chemistry are documented and determine the electrical envelope.


============================================================
3. TEST ENVIRONMENT
============================================================

Testing type:
Controlled laboratory accelerated life testing

Operating environment:
Laboratory-controlled testing conditions

Temperature:
Battery surface temperature is continuously recorded.

Temperature control:
The dataset documentation primarily emphasizes controlled
electrical loading; temperature diversity is not defined as a
primary experimental factor.

Environmental conditions:
No real-world environmental exposure is represented.


============================================================
4. CYCLING PROTOCOL
============================================================

Continuous measurements are recorded throughout the cycling
history of each battery pack.

Operating modes:
- -1: Discharge
-  0: Rest
-  1: Charge

Reference discharge:
Mission type 0 corresponds to a reference discharge performed at
approximately 2.5 A constant current.

Regular mission:
Mission type 1 corresponds to the regular operating mission
assigned to the battery pack.

Regular alternating batteries:
These batteries maintain a consistent load profile throughout
their lifetime.

Recommissioned batteries:
The load level is changed at predefined degradation stages,
typically after the capacity decreases to approximately 2.2 Ah
and, for some batteries, approximately 2.0 Ah.

Second-life batteries:
Surviving cells from previous tests are assembled into new battery
packs and subjected to lower-stress operating profiles.


============================================================
5. LOAD-PROFILE DIVERSITY (regular_alt_batteries folder)
============================================================

The regular folder contains battery packs subject to constant
and variable loading conditions, where the current range of the
latter remains the same throughout the battery life.

Constant-current groups:
* 9.30 A:  battery packs 0.1 and 1.1
* 12.9 A:  battery packs 3.1 and 2.2
* 14.3 A:  battery packs 2.3 and 5.2
* 16.0 A:  battery packs 0.0 and 1.0
* 19.0 A:  battery packs 2.0, 3.0 and 2.1

Variable-current groups:
Those battery packs are subjected to variable current within a
discharge cycle. The current level segments are kept constant for
either 40, 60 or 80 seconds before switching to another level.
Both the amplitude and length of each current level segment were
randomly selected to create a pool of different random loading
discharge missions.

* 14.3 A average (12.9 A - 16 A range): battery packs 4.1 and 5.1
* 17.0 A average (16 A - 19 A range):   battery packs 4.0 and 5.0

The documented discharge-current levels for the regular folder are
therefore approximately: 9.30, 12.9, 14.3, 16.0, 17.0 and 19.0 A
across 7 experimental conditions and 15 packs (mean 2.14 packs per
condition).

Recommissioned profiles (documented for the broader dataset, not
present in the regular folder):
- 16 A -> 14.3 A
- 14.3 A -> 16 A
- 16 A -> 12.9 A
- 16 A -> 9.30 A
- 16 A -> 14.3 A -> 7.5 A
- 16 A -> 7.5 A -> 14.3 A

Second-life profiles (documented for the broader dataset, not
present in the regular folder):
- 16.0 A -> 9.3 A
- 14.3 A -> 7.5 A
- 12.9 A -> 5.0 A


============================================================
6. AVAILABLE DATA
============================================================

File format:
CSV

File organization:
One CSV file per battery pack.

Continuously recorded variables:
- Start time
- Relative time
- Operating mode
- Charger voltage
- Battery temperature

Discharge mission variables:
- Load voltage
- Load current
- MOSFET temperature
- Current-sense resistor temperature
- Mission type

Temporal information:
Continuous relative time is provided from the beginning of
testing until battery failure.

Note on charger voltage:
The "charger voltage" column records the terminal voltage of the
charger, not the pack terminal voltage. It is therefore not
directly comparable to the pack-voltage window and is excluded
from the physical-plausibility check.


============================================================
7. BATTERY STATE AND DEGRADATION
============================================================

Capacity:
Capacity degradation is used to define important experimental
transition points for recommissioned batteries.

Capacity transition points:
- Approximately 2.2 Ah for the first operating-condition change
- Approximately 2.0 Ah for the second change in selected batteries

Failure:
Testing continues over the battery lifetime toward failure.

Degradation information:
The continuous cycling history enables analysis of degradation
under different loading strategies.

SOH:
SOH is not necessarily provided as a directly recorded variable
and may require derivation from capacity-related measurements or
reference discharge data.


============================================================
8. REPRESENTATIVENESS AND DIVERSITY
============================================================

Chemistry diversity:
1 chemistry family (LFP). No chemistry variation between packs.

Pack configuration:
All packs in the regular folder are 2S, ~1.1 Ah per cell.

Temperature diversity:
Not a controlled experimental factor. Battery surface
temperatures vary with ambient and self-heating; there is no
systematic multi-temperature characterisation.

DoD diversity:
Full-depth cycling. Partial-DoD profiles are not part of the
regular-folder design.

C-rate / load-profile diversity:
High. 7 documented discharge conditions across 15 packs, spanning
9.30 A to 19.0 A (approximately 8.5C to 17.3C at cell level on
1.1 Ah nominal capacity), including both constant-current and
variable-current groups.

Replicate cells:
Approximately 2.14 packs per documented discharge condition on
average (7 conditions, 15 packs; some conditions have 2 packs,
one condition has 3).

Dynamic load profiles:
Present. mission_type = 1 (regular/variable mission) is used in
the majority of discharge cycles; mission_type = 0 (reference
constant-current 2.5 A) is used for periodic reference
discharges.

Calendar aging:
Absent as a dedicated experimental condition. All aging is
cyclic; rest periods exist between cycles but are not structured
as calendar-aging experiments.

Real-world operation:
Absent. All testing is laboratory-based. Variable-current
missions simulate dynamic loading but are prescribed laboratory
profiles, not field measurements.


============================================================
9. QUALITY-ASSESSMENT NOTES
============================================================

Design rule:
Hardcode only facts that cannot be determined from the raw
cycle-data files at all. Everything else is computed dynamically.

Physical plausibility:
- Current (cell level):  charge <= 1.0C, discharge <= 18.0C,
  with 1C = 1.1 Ah (LFP cell nominal capacity).
  The 18.0C ceiling is dataset-appropriate: NASA's regular folder
  documents discharge currents up to 19 A pack (= 19 A per cell,
  2S) at 1.1 Ah nominal capacity, i.e. ~17.3C, as a deliberate
  high-rate accelerated-life design. The general 5C envelope
  would flag nearly every cycle, so an 18C ceiling is used to
  catch genuine outliers (sensor spikes, unit errors) without
  flagging the design.
- Temperature (battery surface): -20 to 60 C.
- Voltage: PACK voltage is NOT checked against a fixed envelope
  here, because the charger-side column records charger terminal
  voltage (near 0 V between pulses, above pack max during CV) and
  the load-side column is populated only during discharge/rest.
  There is no always-on pack-voltage column, so a fixed-window
  check would misapply a bound to a column whose semantics do not
  match. This is documented rather than papered over.

Current envelope is applied at cell level. The dataset is a 2S
pack (series), so I_cell = I_pack; pack-level current is used
directly without a parallel divisor.

Documented vs. observed current level:
NASA's README documents seven discharge-current groups for the
regular folder. Each pack's measured mean discharge current is
snapped to its nearest documented level and compared. This is a
protocol cross-check, scored separately from physical
plausibility.

Replicate cells:
Grouped by documented discharge-current level (from the README),
not by data-derived mean current. This respects NASA's stated
experimental design instead of letting small numerical
differences split replicates.

Cycle contribution per pack:
Computed within documented replicate groups at the same
discharge-current level, not pooled across all packs. Pooled CV
would be trivially large by design for this heterogeneous
dataset.
