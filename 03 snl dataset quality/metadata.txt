Degradation of Commercial Lithium-ion Cells as a

Source:
Sandia National Laboratories / Battery Archive
https://batteryarchive.org/


Dataset type:
Laboratory battery degradation dataset

Study duration:
Nearly three years

============================================================

1. DATASET IDENTITY
============================================================

Cell format:
18650 cylindrical cells

Chemistries:
NCA, NMC, and LFP

Chemistry diversity:
3 chemistries

Dataset type:
Laboratory battery degradation dataset

Application:
Battery degradation analysis, SOH estimation, and
cycling-condition analysis

============================================================
2. TEST ENVIRONMENT
===================

Temperature:
15°C, 25°C, and 35°C

Temperature diversity:
3 conditions

Environmental chamber:
SPX Tenney Model T10C-1.5

Battery testers:
Arbin SCTS and Arbin high-precision LBT21084

Cell holders:
18650 battery holders

Temperature measurement:
K-type thermocouple attached to the cell surface

Wiring:
18 gauge wire; cable lengths below 8 feet

============================================================
3. CYCLING PROTOCOL
===================

Pre-test conditioning:
Cells were placed in thermal chambers for
approximately one day to equilibrate to the target
temperature.

Initial condition:
Cells were discharged before cycling.

Capacity check:
Three 0--100% SOC cycles at 0.5C.

Charging:
CCCV charging with taper to 0.05 A for full-depth
cycling.

Cycling rounds:
Approximately 125--1000 cycles at the designated
test conditions.

Post-round check:
Capacity check repeated after each cycling round.

Adaptive cycling:
The number of cycles was reduced when capacity loss
exceeded 5% during the previous round.

End-of-life criterion:
80% of rated capacity.

============================================================
4. CYCLING CONDITION DIVERSITY
==============================

Temperature:
15°C, 25°C, and 35°C

Depth of discharge:
0--100%, 20--80%, and 40--60%

Discharge C-rate:
0.5C, 1C, 2C, and 3C, depending on chemistry

Charge C-rate:
0.5C

Charge protocol:
CCCV for 0--100% DOD and CC for partial-DOD cycling.

Controlled variables:
Temperature, DOD, and discharge rate

Experimental diversity:
Different combinations of chemistry, temperature,
DOD, and discharge rate are tested.

NCA:

NMC:
0.5C, 1C, 2C, and 3C discharge conditions

Voltage limits for 0--100% DOD:

LFP:
2.0--3.6 V

2.5--4.2 V

============================================================
6. DATA
=======

Data format:
CSV

Data types:

* Cycle-level summary data
* High-resolution time-series data

Cycle-level measurements include:

* Cycle index
* Start time
* End time
* Test time
* Minimum current
* Maximum current
* Minimum voltage
* Maximum voltage
* Charge capacity
* Discharge capacity
* Charge energy
* Discharge energy

Time-series measurements include:

* Time
* Voltage
* Current
* Temperature

SOH-related quantity:
Discharge capacity provides the main capacity-based
indicator for degradation and SOH estimation.

============================================================
7. BATTERY DEGRADATION
======================

Ageing mode:
Long-term cycling under controlled conditions.

Capacity degradation:
Tracked through repeated capacity checks and
cycle-level discharge capacity.

End-of-life:
80% of rated capacity.

Adaptive cycling:
Cycling rounds are adjusted according to the observed
capacity loss.

Degradation diversity:
Different degradation trajectories are obtained across
chemistries and cycling conditions.

Cycle contribution:
Varies because cycling duration is adapted according
to degradation.

============================================================
8. REPRESENTATIVENESS AND DIVERSITY
===================================

Chemistry diversity:
3 chemistries (LFP, NCA, and NMC)

Temperature diversity:
3 temperature conditions (15°C, 25°C, and 35°C)

DoD diversity:
3 DOD ranges (0--100%, 20--80%, and 40--60%)

C-rate diversity:
Multiple discharge rates from 0.5C to 3C, depending
on chemistry

Replicate cells/vehicles:
Multiple replicate cells are available for experimental
conditions.

Calendar aging:
Absent as a dedicated experimental condition

Dynamic load profiles:
Absent; cycling uses controlled laboratory protocols.

Real-world operation:
Absent; all testing is performed under laboratory
conditions.
