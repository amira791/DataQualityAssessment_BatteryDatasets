============================================================
300-EV REAL-WORLD BMS DATASET
QUALITY ASSESSMENT METADATA
===========================

Dataset:
300-EV Real-World BMS Dataset

Source:
Liu et al. (2025), Nature Communications

Dataset type:
Large-scale real-world electric vehicle BMS dataset

Dataset purpose:
Real-world battery state estimation, degradation analysis, and
battery health assessment using production electric vehicles.

Dataset availability:
Publicly available dataset and associated implementation code.

============================================================

1. DATASET IDENTITY
   ============================================================

Dataset name:
300-EV Real-World BMS Dataset

Study:
Multi-modal framework for battery state of health evaluation
using open-source electric vehicle data

Publication:
Liu et al. (2025), Nature Communications

Number of vehicles:
300 production electric vehicles

Monitoring duration:
Approximately three years

Battery chemistry:
NCM lithium-ion

Pack configuration:
96 cells in series

Rated pack capacity:
155 Ah

Total monitored cells:
28,800 cells (300 vehicles x 96 cells)

============================================================
2. VEHICLE AND BATTERY CHARACTERISTICS
======================================

Vehicle type:
Production electric vehicles

Battery type:
NCM lithium-ion battery packs

Pack configuration:
96 cells in series

Cell-level monitoring:
Individual voltages of all 96 cells are recorded for each vehicle.

Battery capacity:
Approximately 155 Ah rated capacity per pack.

Fleet scale:
300 vehicles representing real-world vehicle operation.

============================================================
3. DATA ACQUISITION
===================

Acquisition context:
Real-world vehicle operation using onboard battery management
systems.

Sampling frequency:
0.1 Hz, corresponding to approximately one sample every
10 seconds.

Recording behavior:
BMS recording is active during monitored vehicle operation and
stops when the vehicle is switched off.

Operational states:

* Driving
* Charging
* Rest

Environmental conditions:
Uncontrolled ambient conditions encountered during real-world
vehicle operation.

============================================================
4. OPERATING CONDITIONS
=======================

Driving:
Natural user-dependent driving including acceleration,
deceleration, cruising, and regenerative braking.

Driving environments:
City and highway operation.

Charging:
Real-world charging with different charging rates and charging
durations.

Fast charging:
Up to approximately 0.8C.

Slow charging:
Approximately 0.15C.

Charging behavior:
Charging commonly starts below 50% SOC and continues toward full
charge, while partial charging segments are also present.

Temperature:
Naturally varying ambient and battery temperature conditions
across different operating periods, climates, and seasons.

DoD:
Variable and determined by real-world driving and charging behavior.

============================================================
5. AVAILABLE DATA
=================

Raw data:

* Timestamp
* Cumulative mileage
* Pack SOC
* Total current
* Pack voltage
* Highest cell voltage
* Lowest cell voltage
* Individual cell voltages
* Battery temperature

Cell-level measurements:
Voltage measurements are available for all 96 cells in each
battery pack.

Processed data:

* Capacity-based SOH labels
* Charge-capacity sequences (Q_HI)
* Temperature sequences (T_HI)
* Cell-voltage inconsistency maps
* 15 engineered point features

SOH labels:
SOH is obtained using Ah integration with OCV correction.

============================================================
6. REAL-WORLD OPERATION
=======================

Operation type:
Passive observational field data.

Laboratory control:
No controlled cycling protocol is imposed on the vehicles.

Driving diversity:
Natural variation in acceleration, deceleration, cruising,
regenerative braking, traffic conditions, and driving routes.

Charging diversity:
Different charging rates, charging durations, and partial
charging behaviors are naturally represented.

Environmental diversity:
Operation occurs under uncontrolled ambient conditions across
different seasons and climates.

Real-world coverage:
The dataset directly represents production EV operation rather
than laboratory-replayed driving profiles.

============================================================
7. BATTERY STATE AND DEGRADATION
================================

SOH:
Capacity-based SOH labels are provided.

SOH estimation basis:
Ah integration combined with OCV correction.

Capacity information:
Charge-capacity sequences are available for degradation analysis.

Cell-level degradation:
Individual cell voltages enable analysis of cell-to-cell
heterogeneity and voltage inconsistency.

Health-related features:
Cell-voltage inconsistency maps and engineered features are
provided in addition to raw measurements.

Mileage:
Cumulative mileage is continuously recorded and can be used as a
long-term usage indicator.

============================================================
8. REPRESENTATIVENESS AND DIVERSITY
===================================

Chemistry diversity:
One NCM chemistry is represented.

Temperature conditions:
Natural temperature variation is present across real-world
operating conditions, seasons, and climates.

DoD diversity:
High natural variation is expected from user-dependent driving
and partial/full charging behavior.

C-rate diversity:

approximately 0.15C slow charging and up to approximately 0.8C

Replicate vehicles:
300 production vehicles provide a large number of independent
vehicle-level observations.

Calendar aging:
Long-term real-world operation over approximately three years

calendar-aging protocol is imposed.

Dynamic load profiles:
Strong coverage through naturally varying driving and charging
loads.

Real-world operation:
Strong coverage through continuous field operation of production
EVs.

============================================================
11. KNOWN DATA QUALITY CONSIDERATIONS
=====================================

* The dataset is observational rather than experimentally
  controlled.
* Operating conditions therefore vary naturally across vehicles
  and usage patterns.
* BMS recording stops when the vehicle is off, creating periods
  without measurements.
* The dataset contains one primary battery chemistry, limiting
  cross-chemistry assessment.
* Temperature, DoD, and C-rate distributions are naturally
  generated rather than experimentally balanced.
* The amount of data contributed by individual vehicles may vary.
* Cell-level voltage measurements provide high spatial resolution
  but do not imply that all internal cell states are directly
  measured.
* SOH labels are processed values derived from Ah integration and
  OCV correction rather than direct physical measurements.
* Real-world operation introduces heterogeneous and potentially
  unbalanced operating conditions.
