NASA RANDOMIZED AND RECOMMISSIONED BATTERY DATASET
QUALITY ASSESSMENT METADATA
============================================================

Dataset:
Randomized and Recommissioned Battery Dataset

Source:
NASA

Dataset type:
Laboratory accelerated life testing dataset for lithium-ion
battery prognostics and health management

Dataset URL:
https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/randomized-and-recommissioned-battery-dataset


============================================================
1. DATASET IDENTITY
============================================================

Dataset name:
Randomized and Recommissioned Battery Dataset

Provider:
NASA

Dataset purpose:
Accelerated life testing and degradation analysis of lithium-ion
battery packs under controlled and varied load conditions.

Dataset organization:
Each battery pack is provided as a dedicated CSV file containing
the continuous cycling history of the pack.

Main battery categories:
- Regular alternating batteries
- Recommissioned batteries
- Second-life batteries

The dataset includes both conventional constant-current profiles
and variable-current profiles, as well as batteries whose operating
conditions are modified during their lifetime.


============================================================
2. BATTERY CHARACTERISTICS
============================================================

Battery type:
Lithium-ion battery packs

Battery unit:
Battery pack

Number of identified battery packs:
Multiple packs across regular, recommissioned, and second-life
testing categories.

Second-life batteries:
Some packs are reconstructed from cells that survived previous
tests and are subsequently subjected to lower-stress operating
profiles.

Initial battery configuration:
Varies according to the experimental group and battery pack.

Battery chemistry:
Lithium-ion; detailed chemistry information should be verified
from the original NASA documentation where required.


============================================================
3. TEST ENVIRONMENT
============================================================

Testing type:
Controlled laboratory accelerated life testing

Operating environment:
Laboratory-controlled testing conditions

Temperature:
Battery surface temperature is continuously recorded.

Temperature control:
The dataset documentation primarily emphasizes controlled
electrical loading; temperature diversity is not defined as a
primary experimental factor.

Environmental conditions:
No real-world environmental exposure is represented.


============================================================
4. CYCLING PROTOCOL
============================================================

Continuous measurements are recorded throughout the cycling
history of each battery pack.

Operating modes:
- -1: Discharge
-  0: Rest
-  1: Charge

Reference discharge:
Mission type 0 corresponds to a reference discharge performed at
approximately 2.5 A constant current.

Regular mission:
Mission type 1 corresponds to the regular operating mission
assigned to the battery pack.

Regular alternating batteries:
These batteries maintain a consistent load profile throughout
their lifetime.

Recommissioned batteries:
The load level is changed at predefined degradation stages,
typically after the capacity decreases to approximately 2.2 Ah
and, for some batteries, approximately 2.0 Ah.

Second-life batteries:
Surviving cells from previous tests are assembled into new battery
packs and subjected to lower-stress operating profiles.


============================================================
5. LOAD-PROFILE DIVERSITY
============================================================

Constant-current groups:
- Approximately 9.30 A
- Approximately 12.9 A
- Approximately 14.3 A
- Approximately 16.0 A
- Approximately 19.0 A

Variable-current groups:
Current changes randomly between predefined levels with segment
durations of approximately 40, 60, or 80 seconds.

Variable-current group 1:
Approximately 14.3 A average, with current levels between
approximately 12.9 and 16 A.

Variable-current group 2:
Approximately 17.0 A average, with current levels between
approximately 16 and 19 A.

Recommissioned profiles:
- 16 A -> 14.3 A
- 14.3 A -> 16 A
- 16 A -> 12.9 A
- 16 A -> 9.30 A
- 16 A -> 14.3 A -> 7.5 A
- 16 A -> 7.5 A -> 14.3 A

Second-life profiles:
- 16.0 A -> 9.3 A
- 14.3 A -> 7.5 A
- 12.9 A -> 5.0 A

The dataset therefore provides substantial diversity in current
profiles and load transitions.


============================================================
6. AVAILABLE DATA
============================================================

File format:
CSV

File organization:
One CSV file per battery pack.

Continuously recorded variables:
- Start time
- Relative time
- Operating mode
- Charger voltage
- Battery temperature

Discharge mission variables:
- Load voltage
- Load current
- MOSFET temperature
- Current-sense resistor temperature
- Mission type

Temporal information:
Continuous relative time is provided from the beginning of testing
until battery failure.


============================================================
7. BATTERY STATE AND DEGRADATION
============================================================

Capacity:
Capacity degradation is used to define important experimental
transition points for recommissioned batteries.

Capacity transition points:
- Approximately 2.2 Ah for the first operating-condition change
- Approximately 2.0 Ah for the second change in selected batteries

Failure:
Testing continues over the battery lifetime toward failure.

Degradation information:
The continuous cycling history enables analysis of degradation
under different loading strategies.

SOH:
SOH is not necessarily provided as a directly recorded variable
and may require derivation from capacity-related measurements or
reference discharge data.


============================================================
8. REPRESENTATIVENESS AND DIVERSITY
============================================================

Chemistry diversity:
The dataset represents lithium-ion batteries, but multiple
chemistries are not identified as a primary experimental factor.

Temperature conditions:
Temperature is recorded, but multiple controlled temperature
conditions are not the primary source of dataset diversity.

DoD diversity:
Different load profiles and degradation stages are represented,
but explicit DoD levels should be derived or verified from the
recorded operating data.

C-rate diversity:
High diversity is provided through multiple current levels and
variable-current profiles.

Replicate batteries:
Multiple battery packs are tested under related operating
conditions.

Calendar aging:
No dedicated calendar-aging protocol is identified.

Dynamic load profiles:
Strong coverage through randomly varying current profiles and
multi-stage recommissioning protocols.

Real-world operation:
Not represented; the dataset consists of controlled laboratory
accelerated life testing.

Chronological structure:

Lifetime coverage:

Operating-condition transitions:

============================================================
11. KNOWN DATA QUALITY CONSIDERATIONS
============================================================

- The dataset combines several experimental battery categories
  with different testing strategies.
- Operating conditions are intentionally heterogeneous across
  battery packs.
- Variable-current profiles introduce additional temporal
  variability compared with constant-current tests.
- Recommissioned batteries contain deliberate changes in load
  conditions during their lifetime.
- Second-life batteries have a different degradation history
  because they are assembled from cells that survived previous
  testing.
- Temperature is measured at the battery surface and additional
  temperatures are available for the MOSFET and current-sense
  resistor.
- SOH may need to be derived rather than directly read from a
  dedicated SOH signal.
- The dataset is laboratory-based and therefore does not directly
  represent real-world vehicle operation.
