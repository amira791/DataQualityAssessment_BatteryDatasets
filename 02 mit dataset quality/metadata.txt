============================================================
3. CYCLING PROTOCOL
============================================================

Charging:
Fast-charging protocols with one-step or two-step
charging strategies

Charging protocol notation:
C1(Q1)-C2 for two-step protocols

Fast-charge termination:
Charging to approximately 80% SOC, followed by
1C constant-current/constant-voltage charging

Discharging:
Single principal discharge protocol

Discharge rate:
Approximately 4C

Discharge cutoff voltage:
2.0 V

Charge cutoff voltage:
3.6 V

Operating temperature:
30°C


============================================================
4. FAST-CHARGING DIVERSITY
============================================================

Charging strategy:
Multiple fast-charging protocols

Protocol structure:
One-step and two-step charging policies

Charging-rate diversity:
Present

Charging protocols:
Different cells were subjected to different
fast-charging strategies.

Discharge-protocol diversity:
Limited; the dataset uses one principal discharge
strategy across the cells.

Therefore:
Charging diversity should be considered when assessing
C-rate diversity, while the discharge protocol itself
should not be treated as multiple independent discharge
conditions.

- Discharge capacity

============================================================
7. BATTERY DEGRADATION
============================================================

Ageing mode:
Long-term cycling to failure or experimental stopping.

Capacity degradation:
Progressively recorded through cycle-level discharge
capacity.

SOH derivation:
Capacity retention can be calculated from discharge
capacity relative to the initial/reference capacity.

End-of-life reference:
80% capacity retention is commonly used for battery
lifetime analysis; however, the dataset contains cells
with different degradation trajectories and experimental
stopping points.

Cycle-life diversity:
Large variation in the number of cycles achieved across
cells.


============================================================
8. REPRESENTATIVENESS AND DIVERSITY
============================================================

Chemistry diversity:
1 chemistry (LFP/Graphite)

Temperature diversity:
1 temperature condition (30°C)

DoD diversity:
Limited; the principal discharge protocol is common
across the dataset

C-rate diversity:
Present, primarily through multiple fast-charging
protocols

Replicate cells/vehicles:
124 independent cells

Calendar aging:
Absent as a dedicated experimental condition

Dynamic load profiles:
Absent; the principal discharge protocol is controlled
rather than a dynamic real-world load profile

Absent; all cells were tested under laboratory
