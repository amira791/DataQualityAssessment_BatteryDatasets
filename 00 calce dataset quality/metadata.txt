============================================================
7. OFFICIAL CALCE INFORMATION
============================================================

The CALCE documentation describes the CX2 family as prismatic
LiCoO2 cells with a nominal capacity of 1350 mAh. CX2-16 is
listed among the cells cycled at a constant current of 0.5C.

The broader CALCE archive contains additional CX2 cells with
different experimental conditions, including 1C and 3C cycling,
temperature cycling, and pulsed discharge profiles. These
conditions are not attributed to CX2-16 and should therefore
not be used to score the CX2-16 dataset.
